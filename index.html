Who this is for

Members, students, and the friends and families at your table are all welcome to send a photograph or a short video. It does not need to be a good photograph. It needs to be yours.

If you would rather be prompted

The table before anyone sits down. The cook. The youngest and the oldest person in the room. A dish that only your family makes. The drive. The church. The empty chair.

If you have lost someone this year

You are welcome to send a photograph of a cherished memory instead, and we will carry it with the same care. Say so in the caption and we will handle it gently.
