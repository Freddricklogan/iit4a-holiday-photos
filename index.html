Holiday Photos

However your family marks the season, we would be glad to see it. This call is open to every IIT-4A member, to our students, and to the friends and families who sit at your table. Selected photographs and short videos will appear in Newsletter Issue III, published January 31.

Submissions close Saturday, January 30, 2027 at midnight. Photographs and short videos — about a minute is plenty — are both welcome.

Any holiday, Thanksgiving through King Day

Our alumni celebrate all over the world. If your holiday is on this list — or if it isn't — we want the picture.

Thanksgiving · US Native American Heritage Day · US St. Andrew's Day · Scotland Advent begins

Students and alumni both, anywhere in the world. Family tables, church, service days, kitchens, travel, reunions, a quiet morning — whatever the season looks like for you. If we have missed your holiday, name it in the caption and we will name it in the newsletter.

Not sure what to send?

  • The table before anyone sits down, or right after everyone has.
  • Four generations in one frame — the grandparents and the newest baby.
  • Your grandmother's hands, or the one dish that has to be on the table.
  • The Kwanzaa kinara, the menorah, the tree, the altar, the church pew.
  • Serving a meal, packing boxes, or a King Day march.
  • The airport reunion, the driveway hug, the group shot nobody was ready for.
  • Your Illinois Tech sweatshirt somewhere warm.
  • A short video — the blessing, the music, a greeting to the 4A community.

Phone photos are welcome. Send the largest version your phone will give you — print needs the detail.

If this season carries a loss

Many of us will set a place this year for someone who is no longer at the table. If you would like to share a holiday photograph of someone you have lost, please do. We will run it with the care it deserves, and the community will hold that memory with you.

So we can reach you if your photo is selected. Never published.
