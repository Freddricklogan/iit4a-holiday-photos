Holiday Photographs

However your family marks this season, we would be glad to see it.

This call is open to every member of the association, to our students, and to the friends and families who sit at your table. Selected photographs and short videos will appear in Newsletter Issue III.

Submissions close Saturday, January 30, 2027, at midnight. Photographs and short videos are both welcome. Every submission is reviewed by the editor before publication.

Suggestions

If you are not sure what to send

  • The table before anyone sits down, or immediately after everyone has.
  • Four generations in one frame — the grandparents and the newest baby.
  • Your grandmother's hands, or the one dish that has to be on the table.
  • The kinara, the menorah, the tree, the altar, the church pew.
  • Serving a meal, packing boxes, or a King Day march.
  • The airport reunion, the driveway embrace, the group photograph nobody was ready for.
  • Your Illinois Tech sweatshirt somewhere warm.
  • A short video — the blessing, the music, a greeting to the association.

Photographs taken on a telephone are entirely welcome. Please send the largest version your device will give you; print needs the detail.

If this season carries a loss

Many of us will set a place this year for someone who is no longer at the table. If you would like to share a holiday photograph of someone you have lost, please do. We will publish it with the care it deserves, and the association will hold that memory with you.
